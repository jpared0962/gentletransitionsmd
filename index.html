Frequently Asked Questions

Where exactly are the plots?

Block 7, Lot 233, Sites 1, 2, 3, and 4 at Parklawn Memorial Park & Menorah Gardens, in the Wheaton–Rockville area of Montgomery County, Maryland — near the Mausoleum.

Can I buy just one plot?

Yes. Each plot is $3,000 individually, or ask about special package pricing for all four together.

How does the ownership transfer work?

We complete the transfer through the Parklawn cemetery office. Both parties sign the cemetery’s transfer paperwork, the buyer pays the cemetery’s transfer fee, and Parklawn issues a new certificate of interment rights in the buyer’s name.

Are there other costs besides the plot price?

Yes — as with any cemetery purchase, opening and closing (interment) fees are paid to the cemetery at the time of need, plus any memorial or marker you choose.

Do you have plots at other cemeteries?

We buy and sell plots at cemeteries throughout the greater Washington, DC area. Contact us about a specific cemetery.
