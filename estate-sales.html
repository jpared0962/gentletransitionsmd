Estate Sale Questions

How does an estate sale work?

We start with a free consultation at the home, then handle everything: sorting and researching items, fair pricing, staging, advertising, running the sale days, and final cleanout of what remains.

How much does an estate sale cost?

Estate sale companies typically work on commission — a percentage of total sales. Call (240) 758-3226 for a free consultation and a clear explanation of terms before you commit to anything.

How long does setup take?

Most sales take one to three weeks of preparation depending on the home’s size and volume. On a tight timeline? Call us — we can often move faster.

What happens to items that don’t sell?

We discuss this with you up front: donation (with tax receipts), consignment, or cleanout and haul-away — your choice.

Do you buy items outright?

In some cases, yes — for select items or entire small estates we can make a direct cash offer. Ask during your free consultation.

Which areas do you serve?

Montgomery County, Maryland — Wheaton, Silver Spring, Rockville, Bethesda, Takoma Park, Kensington, Chevy Chase, Olney — plus the greater Washington, DC area.
