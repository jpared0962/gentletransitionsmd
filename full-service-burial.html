Full-Service Burial Questions

What exactly does full-service burial include?

The burial plot, all cemetery coordination and scheduling, transfer and interment paperwork, permits and certificates guidance, liaison with your chosen funeral home, and day-of coordination — one point of contact for everything.

Do I still need a funeral home?

Yes — Maryland requires funeral services to go through a licensed funeral establishment. We coordinate directly with the funeral home of your choice, so your family only ever deals with us.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the cemetery, the plot, and the services your family chooses. Call (240) 758-3226 for a clear, itemized quote — no obligation, no pressure.

Which cemeteries do you work with?

Primarily cemeteries across Montgomery County and the greater Washington, DC area, including Parklawn Memorial Park, Gate of Heaven, and Fort Lincoln.

The death has already occurred — can you still help?

Yes. Call us immediately at (240) 758-3226 and we’ll step in and coordinate everything from this point forward.
