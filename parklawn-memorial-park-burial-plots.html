About Parklawn Memorial Park

Parklawn Memorial Park & Menorah Gardens is one of Montgomery County’s most established cemeteries, serving families of all faiths in the Wheaton–Rockville–Silver Spring area for generations. Its landscaped grounds, mausoleums, and dedicated gardens — including Menorah Gardens — make it a sought-after resting place, and desirable sections are not always available directly from the cemetery.

Parklawn Plot Questions

How much do burial plots cost at Parklawn Memorial Park?

Cemetery retail pricing varies by section and availability. These resale plots in Block 7, Lot 233 are $3,000 per plot — typically below what the cemetery charges for comparable sections — with package pricing available for all four.

Can I buy a Parklawn plot from a private seller?

Yes. Parklawn permits private transfers: both parties sign the cemetery’s transfer paperwork at the Parklawn office, the buyer pays the cemetery’s transfer fee, and the cemetery issues a new certificate of interment rights.

What exactly am I buying?

The right of interment (full body burial rights) for each plot. Opening and closing fees are paid to the cemetery at the time of need — standard for every burial plot purchase.
